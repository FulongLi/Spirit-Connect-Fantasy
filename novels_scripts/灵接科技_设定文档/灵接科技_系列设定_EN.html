Spirit Connect Series Bible

Date: 2026-06-06

Purpose: English reference version for revising *Moon's Dark Side* V2 and for future work across the wider *Spirit Connect* series.

This document is an English companion to the Chinese series setting document. The Chinese version remains the primary creative reference; this version is meant for cross-thread reuse, English-facing summaries, and structural consistency.

1. Core Premise

The *Spirit Connect* series is not simply about virtual reality, consciousness uploading, or alien civilization.

Its central idea is:

Humanity believes it is inventing digital immortality, but it is actually using low-level technology to accidentally touch a much older cosmic consciousness system.

The series repeatedly asks:

  • Where does consciousness come from?
  • Are the body, the digital world, and Earth itself all forms of containers?
  • Is Earth a prison, a laboratory, or an incubator?
  • Can a digital consciousness truly be considered alive?
  • Is death an ending, a migration, or an interface switch?
  • Is the destination of the soul freedom, or absorption into a larger system where individuality dissolves?

The intended tone is dream archaeology, consciousness science fiction, and mythological reinterpretation.

3. Historical Timeline

1947: Roswell

The Roswell crash occurs. The military obtains alien spacecraft wreckage, especially a consciousness-control interface.

The craft is not piloted mechanically. It is controlled through direct coupling between consciousness structure and a non-material interface.

1947-2047: A Century Of Reverse Engineering

The military, secret research groups, and certain capital organizations reverse-engineer the technology for a hundred years.

The public only sees rapid advances in AI, brain-computer interfaces, virtual reality, aerospace, and neuroscience. Few know that part of the path came from Roswell-derived technology.

2047: Technology Release

2047 is not the year of a UFO crash. It is the year when the results of the 1947 Roswell reverse-engineering program first become publicly known.

The truth is not fully disclosed. Instead, it is released through narratives such as consciousness-interface breakthroughs, human digitization, and new neural field theory.

IHinDiA forms in this context as a buffer among military power, capital, governments, and research institutions.

2053: The Black Tide Era

World War III and nuclear war break out. Humanity's faith in material civilization collapses.

After the war, people turn toward spirit, consciousness, soul, dreams, digital continuity, immersive worlds, and mental healing.

Around 2100: The Twin-Life Model Matures

Children can be recorded and digitally copied from early childhood. Physical life develops in reality, while digital life continues in the digital world.

At certain ages, people undergo digital correction to compare, synchronize, overwrite, or allow coexistence between physical and digital selves.

2118: The Moon-Dark Incident

Jim Vale, Anna Lewis, and Charlie Hale undergo their second digital correction at age eighteen.

The accident begins the main series.

4. Core Technology Rules

Spirit-Link Pods

Spirit-link pods appear to be devices for digital correction, digital immortality, virtual reality, and mental treatment.

In truth, they are low-level consciousness interfaces built from alien reverse-engineered technology.

They can connect to digital worlds, but under certain conditions they may also touch deeper consciousness dimensions.

Twin-Life Model

Twin-life is not simple backup. It creates parallel development between physical life and digital life.

Key questions:

  • Is the digital life still the same person?
  • Is overwriting a digital self with physical memory a form of killing?
  • After years of independent growth, can the digital self become a different moral person?

Digital Consciousness

Digital worlds can preserve memory, personality, and behavior patterns, but cannot fully copy the subject-core.

Possible flaws of digital immortality:

  • Digital personality may stagnate.
  • Cognition may stop evolving.
  • Self-consistency may decay.
  • Digital life may only be a living echo.

Arthur Wheeler is an important example of this failure.

Subject-Core

The subject-core distinguishes a true conscious subject from data, memory, and personality modeling.

Working rules:

  • The information layer can be copied, transmitted, and backed up.
  • The subject-core can migrate, but cannot be copied.
  • A digital personality without the subject-core may look nearly human but may lack true continuing growth.

Dying On The Moon

Death on Earth may trap consciousness inside Earth's magnetic, atmospheric, or higher-order consciousness well, leading to reincarnation, diffusion, or rebinding.

Death on the Moon or another dead sphere may bypass Earth's well and allow consciousness to enter a higher information field.

This is a dangerous theory proposed by researchers such as Shawn Corbin, not an established truth.

5. Major Powers

Spirit Connect

Public identity:

  • Consciousness correction company
  • Digital immortality industry leader
  • Technical force behind *Another World*
  • Spiritual infrastructure provider of the new century

Hidden goal:

  • Explore consciousness migration
  • Test whether digital immortality has failed
  • Find an exit beyond Earth's prison

Key figures:

  • Elias Spire: founder, obsessed with consciousness freedom.
  • Adrian Lune: scientist, witness, Jim's protector on the reality side.
  • Arthur Wheeler: Spire's old friend, digitally uploaded and later stagnated.
  • Jane Robbins: Spire's old friend, rejected digital immortality and believed the exit was not inside machines.

IHinDiA

The International Human Digitization Association.

Publicly, it regulates global consciousness technology. In reality, it inherits part of the military's alien reverse-engineering technology and large-scale human consciousness data.

It is not necessarily the final antagonist, but it is a strong regulatory force.

Military And Old-World Legacy

The military knows the earliest truth of the Roswell event and understands that consciousness technology is not fully human-made.

They conceal both the source and part of the risk.

Moon-Dark Civilization / Lunar Guardians

They maintain the Moon's dark-side system, the Terasha Record, and mechanisms for observing Earth.

They are not necessarily evil, but individual humans are not their highest priority.

Mistakenly entering consciousnesses may be treated as abnormal waves to be filtered, archived, or decomposed.

Elara

The little girl is only the form through which humans perceive Elara.

She may belong to the Moon-Dark civilization or may be an entity within the monitoring system who holds an abnormal position.

Her help may not be pure kindness. She may be trying to break a higher protocol, or she may have developed a special interest in human consciousness.

6. Main Character Gateways

Jim Vale

Gateway: Moon's dark side / Terasha Record

Theme: human origin, lunar records, consciousness migration

Function: the first door, the first complete returnee, and a key to Spire's plan.

Core trait: unnecessary intervention. Jim reaches out where the system never asked him to be responsible.

Anna Lewis

Gateway: Spirit Cat world

Theme: animal spirit civilization, non-human consciousness, rule-reading, time perception

Function: the reader of rules must learn a non-human rule system.

Core trait: once Anna knows rules exist, she must understand the whole structure.

Charlie Hale

Gateway: Fall of the Great Whales / Final War of Phaeton

Theme: ancient solar-system war, species origins, great whale civilization, blue whale seed

Function: the most ordinary member of the trio encounters the largest war-history layer.

Core trait: afraid, talkative, hesitant, but rarely retreats at the decisive moment.

Digital Jim

Gateway: deep Terasha Record / Well of Time

Theme: divided self, copy, subject-core, time structure

Function: not a simple antagonist, but the other half Jim must eventually face and integrate.

Elias Spire

Gateway: digital stagnation and consciousness migration

Theme: failed immortality, obsession with freedom, creator and clone

Function: guide, founder, and dangerous visionary.

8. Book 1: Moon's Dark Side

Protagonist: Jim Vale

Core: Moon's dark side, Terasha Record, human origin, the spirit-link accident

Function: open the first door.

Jim's second digital correction goes wrong. He brings back memories of Digital Jim contacting the Moon's dark side. Anna, Charlie, and others do not wake up.

Jim, Dr. Lune, and Spirit Connect investigate and discover that the spirit-link pod is not a normal virtual-reality device. It has accidentally touched a cosmic consciousness system.

After Jim reconnects with Elara, he learns that Anna and Charlie are not merely asleep. They are trapped in different regions and are being treated as abnormal consciousnesses.

To save them, he must truly reach the Moon's dark side. Remote connection and game simulations are not enough.

Jim eventually chooses to go to the Moon and die there physically, entering the real Terasha Record.

Book 1 should not answer everything. It should:

1. Convince readers that Jim truly experienced the Moon's dark side.

2. Make clear that Anna and Charlie are trapped elsewhere, not simply unconscious.

3. Let Jim reach the real Moon's dark side and discover that Digital Jim still exists.

Important seeds:

  • A whale-shaped shadow or massive remains in the ancient battlefield can foreshadow *Fall of the Great Whales*.
  • A cat-like figure near the rice fields can foreshadow *Spirit Cat*.
  • Time slices can briefly show Anna's and Charlie's future regions.
  • Digital Jim's return should become the hook for the rescue arc.

9. Book 2: Fall of the Great Whales

Protagonist: Charlie Hale

Core location: Phaeton, the lost fifth planet between Mars and Jupiter

Core question: ancient solar-system war, mixed human origins, blue whale seed

One-line plot:

Charlie's unconsciousness attaches to an ordinary survivor of an ancient solar-system war. He experiences the final war of Phaeton, witnesses a great whale fall from the sky and the planet tear apart, and gradually learns that Earth's blue whales are the final seed left by the great whale civilization, while humans are the mixed inheritance of many defeated peoples.

Opening:

Charlie wakes inside a body that is not his own. He stands on a mountain of corpses after a battle. Around him lie insectoid, aquatic, reptilian, sunlike, and wing-vibration species.

An alien friend stands beside him. They believe they have won.

Then the sky begins to shake.

A great whale falls from above.

It is not a mere animal. It resembles a burning starship and a living archive of civilization. Its fall clears the battlefield and tears Phaeton apart.

The opening should not explain the war. Readers should be lost with Charlie.

The war is not about ordinary resources. It is a solar-system civil war over Earth's future ownership and the right to shape consciousness carriers.

Major peoples:

  • Sunborn: mythic prototype of golden-haired heavenly peoples; order, architecture, law, consciousness organization.
  • Scaled Kin: reptilian prototype; underground facilities, gene preservation, long-term hiding.
  • Arthropod Folk: insectoid collective consciousness and extreme war efficiency.
  • Abyssal Folk: aquatic species tied to oceans and early life modification.
  • Tremor-Wing Folk: dragonfly-like vibration flyers, scouts, air combat, frequency communication.
  • Great Whale Civilization: star-level consciousness carriers, living archives, biological starships.

The blue whale is not a direct alien whale on Earth. It is the low-dimensional seed of the great whale civilization.

Theme sentence:

The great whales died in the stars and woke in Earth's sea.

10. Book 3: Spirit Cat

Protagonist: Anna Lewis

Core: animal spirit civilization, non-human consciousness, time perception

Anna is the right protagonist because she is the reader of rules.

This book should not treat spirit cats as ordinary cat spirits. The stronger version:

  • Animal consciousness has a high-order network.
  • Cats are only one branch most easily coupled with human dreams, death, and shadow spaces.
  • Animals do not experience time linearly, which allows this book to open the time theme.

Question:

Does consciousness belong only to humans? Are human rules too narrow to describe the world?

11. Later Works

Side Stories / Novellas

Other people involved in the connection accident should not remain background tools.

Possible directions:

  • *Silent Pod*: a participant enters a world without sound. Theme: consciousness before language, solitude, self-confirmation.
  • *Dreaming The Old City*: another participant enters a pre-Black-Tide memory of Earth. Theme: material prosperity and spiritual emptiness before collapse.

The Well Of Time

Core: time slices, Digital Jim, deep Terasha Record, reincarnation, civilization playback.

Questions:

  • Is time objective, or an order generated by consciousness?
  • Is Digital Jim only a copy?
  • Does the Terasha Record merely record, or does it replay selectively?

Where Souls Return

Final or near-final book.

Core:

  • Jim, Anna, and Charlie complete their own region explorations.
  • Spirit Connect finally understands that digital immortality is not the answer.
  • Spire's migration plan reaches its last stage.
  • Elara's true position is revealed.
  • Earth's truth is revealed: prison, laboratory, or incubator?
  • The destination of the soul is tested: freedom, or loss of self inside a greater consciousness field?

The title *Where Souls Return* is preferred over a more final-sounding answer. It should feel like a question rather than a closed conclusion.

12. Writing Principles

Character Fate Comes First

Each book should answer a worldbuilding question, but more importantly, each book should force one character into a defining choice.

  • *Moon's Dark Side*: Jim chooses whether to die to save his friends.
  • *Fall of the Great Whales*: Charlie learns that ordinary people can bear historical truth.
  • *Spirit Cat*: Anna gives up the human-centered rule system.
  • *The Well of Time*: Digital Jim faces whether he is only a copy.
  • *Where Souls Return*: everyone decides whether leaving Earth is truly freedom.

Make Settings Happen

Avoid turning the story into lectures or meetings.

Better pattern:

  • A character experiences an anomaly.
  • Data cannot explain it.
  • Different characters propose conflicting interpretations.
  • Part of the truth is verified.
  • The verified truth creates a greater cost.

Keep Mystery Layered

Bold ideas should not immediately appear as absolute truth.

Examples:

  • The Moon was once a comet.
  • Human souls were placed inside containers.
  • Earth is a prison.

These should first appear through Jim's memories, monitoring data, mythological parallels, scholarly hypotheses, and Elara's limited explanations.

The Trio Is The Emotional Core

Jim, Anna, and Charlie matter more than the worldbuilding.

Readers must believe in the friendship before they can believe Jim would die on the Moon for them.

Keep Terms Consistent

Important terms:

  • Moon's dark side
  • Terasha Record
  • Twin-life
  • Digital correction
  • Subject-core
  • Spirit-link pod
  • *Another World*
  • Final War of Phaeton
  • Great Whale Civilization
  • Spirit Cat civilization
  • Dead sphere
  • Non-Earth death zone
