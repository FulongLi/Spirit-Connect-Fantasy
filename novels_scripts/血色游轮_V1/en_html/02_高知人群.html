02 The Educated Crowd

Status：framework seed

Main task

Introduce the early relationship and philosophical differences among Spire, Arthur, and Jane.

Secondary task

Carry the chapter's second function while preserving the book's body, machine, and soul questions.

POV limit

Close third person centered on Elias Spire unless the chapter note states otherwise; do not let him know future series truths.

Required characters

Elias Spire, Arthur Wheeler, Jane Robbins, and chapter-specific supporting figures.

Required information

The reader should understand the chapter's position in Spire's movement from faith in reason, to distrust of embodied humanity, to Spirit Connect.

Forbidden mistakes

Do not turn this into encyclopedia exposition. Let setting and philosophy emerge through scenes, choices, and wounds.

Scene Segments

  1. 亚瑟以技术乐观主义者姿态出场，认为上传只是工程难题。
  2. 简以敏锐、安静、带反问的方式出场，她关心机器无法测量的部分。
  3. 三人在酒会边缘谈到意识、肉体和死亡，气氛轻松，但问题很深。

Prose Seed

亚瑟·惠勒说话时总像在拆一台还没发明出来的机器。

简·罗宾斯听他说完，低头看着杯中慢慢消失的气泡，只问了一句：如果机器保留了你的声音，却没有保留你害怕沉默的方式，那还算你吗？

斯皮尔当时笑了。他喜欢这种问题，因为它足够锋利，也足够遥远。
