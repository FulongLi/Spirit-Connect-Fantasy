08 The Beastliness of Reason

Status：framework seed

Main task

Let Spire see reason serving animal instinct.

Secondary task

Carry the chapter's second function while preserving the book's body, machine, and soul questions.

POV limit

Close third person centered on Elias Spire unless the chapter note states otherwise; do not let him know future series truths.

Required characters

Elias Spire, Arthur Wheeler, Jane Robbins, and chapter-specific supporting figures.

Required information

The reader should understand the chapter's position in Spire's movement from faith in reason, to distrust of embodied humanity, to Spirit Connect.

Forbidden mistakes

Prose Seed

他们说得非常轻。

没有人说放弃。没有人说杀死。那些词都太粗糙，不适合出现在这群人的嘴里。

他们说资源倾斜，说整体存活率，说高价值样本，说不可逆损耗。斯皮尔听着，忽然觉得语言本身也会洗手。
